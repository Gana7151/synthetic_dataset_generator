*# 📄 Dataset Content Production – Project Requirements*

*## 1. Project Overview*

We are required to create **synthetic individual tax datasets** for the following U.S. states:

* California
* Texas
* New York
* Illinois
* Florida

Each dataset must be **realistic, internally consistent, and fully structured** as per the sample provided.

---

*## 2. Dataset Distribution*

Datasets must be categorized into three difficulty levels:

* **Level 1 (Easy)**
* **Level 2 (Medium)**
* **Level 3 (Moderately Complex)**

Each level should reflect increasing complexity in terms of:

* Number of documents
* Income types
* Tax scenarios

---

*## 3. Tax Year Coverage*

The dataset must cover **5 historical tax years**:

* 2020
* 2021

* 2024

*## 4. Input Prompt (Client Intake Questionnaire)*

Each dataset must include a **fully completed client intake**, covering:

### Personal & Family Details

* Personal information
* Filing status
* Dependents

### Income Details

* Employment income
* Stock compensation
* Interest and dividends
* Retirement income
* 1099 income (NEC/MISC)
* K-1 income

### Additional Income Sources

* Rental income
* Foreign income
* State disability / unemployment
* ACA marketplace forms

### Deductions & Credits

* Itemized deductions
* Tax credits

### State-Specific Rules

Include rules applicable to:

* California
* Texas
* New York
* Illinois
* Florida

Examples:

* State credits (e.g., CalEITC, YCTC)
* Renter’s credit
* State disability insurance
* Mental health tax (if applicable)

### Compliance Information

* Prior-year tax compliance
* Estimated tax payments

---

*## 5. Supporting Documentation*

Each dataset must include **4–15 realistic supporting documents**, such as:

* W-2
* 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, 1099-B
* 1099-R
* 1099-NEC / 1099-MISC
* Schedule K-1
* ACA Form 1095-A
* Mortgage Interest (Form 1098)
* Property tax bills
* Brokerage statements
* Rental agreements & expense receipts
* Business expense receipts
* Bank statements
* HSA / IRA contribution statements

All documents must be:

* Internally consistent
* Properly formatted
* Fully aligned with the dataset

---

*## 6. Completed Tax Return Forms (Solution)*

Each dataset must include **fully completed and reconciled tax returns**:

### Federal Forms:

* Form 1040
* Schedules 1–3
* Schedule A, B, C, D, E, SE (as applicable)
* Form 4562 (Depreciation)
* Form 8949 + Schedule D (Capital Gains)
* Form 8606 (IRA)
* Additional forms:

  * Form 2441 (Childcare)
  * Form 8863 (Education)
  * Form 8812 (ACTC)
  * Form 1116 (Foreign Tax Credit)

---

### State Forms (as applicable):

* State tax form (e.g., Form 540 or equivalent)
* State Schedule CA or equivalent
* Applicable state credit forms
* Form 3805V (Estimated tax vouchers)

---

*## 7. Executive Summary*

Each dataset must include a **summary section** with:

### Federal Summary:

* Adjusted Gross Income (AGI)
* Taxable Income
* Total Tax
* Total Payments / Credits
* Refund or Payment Due
* Penalties / Interest
* Effective Tax Rate

### State Summary:

* Taxable Income
* Total Tax
* Payments / Credits
* Refund or Balance Due
* Penalties / Interest

---

*## 8. De-Identification Requirements*

All datasets must strictly follow data privacy guidelines:

* Use **synthetic or anonymized data only**
* No real personally identifiable information (PII)
* Apply **multi-stage validation and review**
* Ensure compliance with data protection standards
* Use only approved templates for reference
